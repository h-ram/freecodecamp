Three Horizons

Stone, snow and salt water: a walk through three landscapes.

The Colosseum, Rome

Nearly two thousand years old, the amphitheatre still holds the evening light in its worn arches.

The Alps

Cold air, sharp ridgelines and a silence that makes every footstep on the snow audible.

The Open Sea

Where water and sky blur into one line, and the day seems to slow down.

On view through the end of the season.
